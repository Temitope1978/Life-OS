03

Buttons

Rounded, quiet, single-purpose. Primary for the one next thing, secondary for alternatives, ghost for tertiary actions, danger reserved for destructive confirmations.

Reserved confirmations: “Approve sending this draft?” → Primary · “Cancel” → Secondary · “Delete permanently” → Danger.

04

Inputs & Controls

Labeled, forgiving, keyboard-first. Every control scales to a 44px touch target and exposes a clear focus ring.

Level 5 — only pre-approved, low-risk actions.
